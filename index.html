About the Project

The FERROWORKER will be a 1m x 1m x 1m build size 3D printer that can be assembled in 5 days, with the target builder being 14. We will make a full guide to assembly.

02

Tool Changer

The project will have a tool changer built into one, featuring:

  • 3D Printer
  • Laser Cutter
  • CNC Drill

Recycle Your 3D Printer

Have an old, broken, or unused 3D printer? We will take it, harvest the good parts, and put them toward future FERROWORKER builds. This keeps e-waste out of landfill and lowers the cost of open-source 3D printing for everyone.

  • What we accept
    FDM printers, extruders, hotends, stepper motors, rods, bearings, belts, and electronics. Broken or incomplete is fine.
  • What we cannot accept
    Resin vats, IPA-contaminated parts, or machines soaked in hazardous chemicals.
  • Shipping policy
    Australia: we pay for shipping. International: sender pays for shipping. We will provide a shipping label or drop-off details after confirming the donation.
  • Drop-off / mailing address
    Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. Exact address will be provided by email after your submission is accepted.
  • Contact
    Email ferroworks@outlook.com.au. Please include photos and a short description of the printer.
